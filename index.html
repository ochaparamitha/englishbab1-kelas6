Vegetables & Expressing Likes

Pelajari kosakata nama-nama sayuran, ciri-cirinya, serta aturan kalimat suka (like/likes) dan tidak suka (don't/doesn't like).

🥦
1

Aturan Like, Likes, Don't Like, & Doesn't Like

Digunakan untuk mengungkapkan perasaan suka atau tidak suka terhadap sayuran.

Kalimat Suka (Positive)

  • I, You, We, They ➜ gunakan LIKE
    Contoh: I like carrots. / We like cucumbers.
  • He, She, It ➜ gunakan LIKES (tambah -s)
    Contoh: She likes spinach. / He likes tomatoes.

Kalimat Tidak Suka (Negative)

  • I, You, We, They ➜ gunakan DON'T LIKE
    Contoh: I don't like cucumbers.
  • He, She, It ➜ gunakan DOESN'T LIKE
    Contoh: He doesn't like broccoli.
Aturan Penting Penggunaan Nama Orang:
  • Satu Orang (misal: Rina / Budi) ➜ Dianggap sama seperti He/She (Gunakan likes / doesn't like).
    Contoh: Rina likes spinach. / Budi doesn't like broccoli.
  • Lebih Dari Satu Orang (misal: Rina and Budi) ➜ Dianggap sama seperti They (Gunakan like / don't like).
    Contoh: Rina and Budi like tomatoes. / Rina and Budi don't like onions.
2

Describing Vegetables (Mendeskripsikan Sayuran)

Karakteristik tekstur, warna, bentuk, dan ciri umum sayuran.

Tekstur (Texture)
  • • Hard (Keras)
  • • Soft (Lembut)
  • • Rough (Kasar)
  • • Smooth (Halus)
Ciri Umum
  • • Crunchy (Renyah)
  • • Leafy (Berdaun)
  • • Seeded (Berbiji)
  • • Starchy (Berpati)
  • • Spicy (Pedas)
  • • Layered (Berlapis)
Warna (Color)
  • • Orange, Green
  • • Purple, Yellow
  • • Brown, Red
  • • White
Bentuk (Shape)
  • • Long (Panjang)
  • • Round (Bulat)
  • • Tree-like (Pohon)
  • • Curly (Keriting)
